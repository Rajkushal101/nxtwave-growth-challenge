# Final Project Submission Description

---

## Project Title
**“WHAT AI PROJECT SHOULD YOU BUILD?”**  
*NxtWave 60-Minute AI Project Challenge & Viral Growth Engine*

---

## One-Line Description
> An AI-powered project discovery and growth platform that recommends personalized AI projects to engineering students across all 4 years and turns those recommendations into a measurable, referral-driven acquisition loop.

---

## Problem Statement
Engineering students across India understand that AI skills are mandatory for placements and career readiness, yet the vast majority suffer from **tutorial paralysis** and **relevance mismatch**:
- 1st-year students get overwhelmed by complex dependencies and math.
- 2nd and 3rd-year students find introductory tutorials too trivial to impress recruiters.
- Generic webinar landing pages treat all students identically, resulting in low attendance and poor conversion.

---

## The Solution
Instead of a generic landing page, the platform implements a **value-first growth mechanism**:
1. **Interactive 60-Second Discovery:** Captures academic year, branch, coding proficiency, and career goals.
2. **Dual-Tier Tailored Recommendations:** Pairs Google Gemini 1.5 Flash personalization with a 24-entry deterministic fallback matrix to deliver an achievable 60-minute project from a curated 17-project catalog.
3. **High-Intent Conversion:** The student enrolls in the live workshop *"Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes"* with contextual profile pre-fill.
4. **Viral Growth Mechanism:** Generates a unique referral link (`/r/{CODE}`) and a shareable HTML5 project card with embedded QR code. Friends join, discover their own personalized projects, and close the viral loop.
5. **Growth & Telemetry Cockpit:** Complete administrative instrumentation tracking an 8-stage canonical funnel, Single First-Touch UTM attribution, engineering demographic segmentation, A/B copy experiments, and ₹2,000 budget decision modeling.

---

## Target Audience
- **1st Year (Freshers):** Beginners seeking fast wins and zero-setup exploration.
- **2nd Year (Foundations):** Students transitioning from fundamentals to applied AI applications.
- **3rd Year (Placements):** Pre-final year students seeking standout portfolio projects.
- **4th Year (Capstones):** Final year students seeking deployment-ready capstones.

---

## Core Growth Story & Value Loop
```
GIVE VALUE FIRST (Tailored Project Match)
       ↓
CREATE CURIOSITY ("Build this in 60 mins live")
       ↓
CONVERT HIGH-INTENT (Pre-filled Registration)
       ↓
EMPOWER VIRAL SHARING (Project Card as Social Object)
       ↓
REFERRED PEERS ENTER (/r/CODE Context Preserved)
       ↓
MEASURE TELEMETRY (Canonical 9-Stage Funnel & Drop-Offs)
       ↓
OPTIMIZE SYSTEM (A/B Testing & Data-Informed Budgeting)
```

---

## Key Differentiators
1. **Value-First Architecture:** Delivers the personalized project *before* making the registration ask.
2. **Deterministic Fallback Resilience:** Dual-tier architecture guarantees 100% project delivery even upon complete AI API failure.
3. **The Project as a Social Object:** Friends share what they are building rather than spamming a generic link.
4. **Data Honesty & Partitioning:** Strictly segregates live user telemetry from simulated 7-day demo cohorts.
5. **Actionable Growth Economics:** Every dashboard metric links directly to a concrete hypothesis and ₹2,000 budget decision.
